## The wallet's requests (not the page's)

The extension asked one host, `ootle-indexer-a.tari.com`: 70 requests in the whole session (63 GET, 7 POST). The send itself made 19 of them, from the opening of the approval window to 4 s after the approval. They included two lookups of its own payment by id (`GET /transactions/<id>/result?include_proof=false`). The net log held no scan of the recent transactions during or after the send. Its 29 scans came earlier, from opening the wallet's own screen and pressing Rescan, and one `GET /identity` came when the wallet opened.

## The browser's cache

A plain `fetch` of the list every 20 s, in a fresh Chromium, reached the network at calls 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 and came from the browser's cache at calls 2, 4, 6 and 8. The indexer's answer carries `Cache-Control` with `max-age=30`. The page's own look 2 was not run in this send, because look 1 found the payment. After this send the page's looks pass `cache: 'no-store'` to `fetch`; the unit tests check that the option is passed. NOT VERIFIED: that a real browser then reaches the real indexer at every look.
